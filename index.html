How it works

We do a draw every Friday. You pick six numbers from 1–49. If they match, you win. If they don't, you've spent the week dreaming. That counts for something.

01

Pick your numbers

Choose six from 1 to 49. Or let the machine choose — we call that a Quick Pick. No strategy required. No lucky numbers necessary. Just pick what feels right.

02

Choose your draws

Enter for one draw, four, or twelve. More draws mean more Fridays of anticipation. The ticket is small. The week ahead is where the value is.

03

Wait for Friday

Draws happen Friday at 20:00. We publish the results here, verified. If your numbers come up, we find you. If they don't, there's always next week — and nothing lost but hope renewed.

Our promise

We take the lottery seriously. That means transparency, integrity, and care. Here's what we stand for.

Verified draws

Every draw is independently verified. Results are published here within minutes. If there's a discrepancy, we pause until it's resolved. That's the rule.

Winners are paid

Prizes are held in trust. When you win, the money is there. We don't gamble with your winnings. Every €12 and every €32 million — paid in full.

Play responsibly

You should never spend more than you're comfortable losing. If it stops being fun, stop. There's always another draw. We'll be here when you come back.

No pressure

We don't send push notifications. We don't chase you to play. You decide when and how often. The lottery is entertainment, not a plan.

If gambling is affecting you or someone you know, help is available:

  • Gambling Therapy
  • BeGambleAware
